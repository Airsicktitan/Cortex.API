# Pilot Plan (Landing First Customer)

## Purpose

Convert interest → paid customer

---

## Pilot Goal

Prove:

- reduced follow-ups
- faster decisions
- clearer ownership

---

## Ideal Pilot Target

- 1 team
- 5–15 users
- high ticket volume
- visible operational pain

---

## Pilot Duration

2–4 weeks

---

## Step 1 — Setup

- onboard users
- configure:
  - roles
  - routing rules
  - SLA
- load sample or real tickets

---

## Step 2 — Baseline Metrics

Before using Cortex, measure:

- avg comments per ticket
- avg time to resolution
- SLA breach rate

---

## Step 3 — Run Cortex

Use:

- AI intake assist
- routing
- rebalance
- metrics tracking

---

## Step 4 — Monitor

Track:

- readiness rate
- comment reduction
- SLA risk changes
- routing overrides

---

## Step 5 — Weekly Checkpoint

Ask:

- Are tickets clearer?
- Are decisions faster?
- Are fewer follow-ups needed?

---

## Step 6 — Show Results

At end of pilot, present:

### Before vs After

- comments per ticket ↓
- readiness ↑
- SLA risk ↓

---

## Step 7 — Close

Say:

"This is the operational improvement Cortex delivers."

Then:

"Let’s roll this out to your team."

---

## Pricing Strategy in Pilot

- free OR discounted
- convert based on results

---

## Common Objections

### "We already have a ticketing system"

Response:
"Cortex enhances decision-making, not replaces your system."

---

### "We don’t need AI"

Response:
"AI improves input quality — decisions still follow your rules."

---

### "This seems complex"

Response:
"We start small — one team, measurable impact."

---

## Success Criteria

Pilot is successful if:

- follow-ups decrease
- clarity increases
- team prefers Cortex workflow

---

## Expansion Path

After pilot:

- expand to more teams
- expand to more workflows
- introduce advanced features

---

## Golden Rule

Do not sell features.

Sell:

"less friction, faster decisions, measurable impact"
