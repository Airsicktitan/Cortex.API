# Sales Objections

## Purpose

Handle common objections with clarity and confidence.

---

## Objection 1

"We already use a ticketing system"

Response:

"Cortex doesn’t replace your system—it improves decision-making on top of it."

---

## Objection 2

"We don’t need AI"

Response:

"AI improves input quality. Decisions still follow your rules."

---

## Objection 3

"This seems complex"

Response:

"We start with one team and show measurable impact quickly."

---

## Objection 4

"What’s the ROI?"

Response:

"If this reduces even one follow-up per ticket, you save hours every week."

---

## Objection 5

"How is this different?"

Response:

"Most systems track work. Cortex improves how decisions are made."

---

## Golden Rule

Do not defend features.

Reframe to value.
