# Demo Playbooks

## Purpose

Playbooks define EXACT demo flows.

They ensure:

- consistency across demos
- clear storytelling
- repeatable impact
- no “wandering demos”

---

## Golden Rule

Do NOT improvise the demo structure.

You can adjust tone, but not flow.

---

## Playbook 1 — Core Value Demo (5–7 minutes)

### Goal

Show:

- bad input → improved input
- clarity → faster decisions
- routing → explainability
- measurable value

---

### Step 1 — Create a Bad Ticket

Example:

"SAP not working"

Say:
"This is how tickets typically come in."

---

### Step 2 — Show the Problem

Highlight:

- missing details
- unclear ownership
- no actionable context

Say:
"A reviewer cannot act on this without follow-up."

---

### Step 3 — Use AI Intake Assist

Click:
"Improve Request"

Show:

- missing details surfaced
- improved structure

Say:
"Instead of guessing, the system identifies what's missing immediately."

---

### Step 4 — Show Readiness

Highlight:

- Ready vs Needs Detail
- structured clarity

Say:
"The key question: Can I act on this immediately?"

---

### Step 5 — Show Routing

Open:
Cortex Decision panel

Highlight:

- selected owner
- reasons
- alternatives

Say:
"This isn’t just assignment — it’s explainable routing."

---

### Step 6 — Show Impact

Highlight:

- workload balancing
- SLA improvement

Say:
"This decision reduces risk and distributes work intelligently."

---

### Step 7 — Show Rebalance

Navigate to:
Rebalance panel

Highlight:

- suggested moves
- expected impact

Say:
"The system continuously corrects imbalance."

---

### Step 8 — Show Metrics

Navigate to:
Reports / Metrics

Highlight:

- follow-up reduction
- readiness improvement

Say:
"This is where the business value shows up."

---

### Close

Say:

"This reduces follow-ups, speeds decisions, and makes ownership explicit."

---

## Playbook 2 — Executive Demo (3–5 minutes)

### Goal

Sell value quickly.

---

### Flow

1. Show Dashboard / Executive Attention
2. Show one ticket (clean, ready)
3. Show routing explanation
4. Show metrics

---

### Key Message

"This gives leadership visibility and control over operations."

---

## Playbook 3 — Routing Deep Dive

### Goal

Sell technical credibility.

---

### Show:

- rule matching
- workload scoring
- alternatives
- override behavior

---

### Say:

"This is deterministic, explainable, and auditable."

---

## Playbook 4 — AI Trust Demo

### Goal

Show AI is safe.

---

### Show:

- AI suggestions
- structured output
- manual apply

---

### Say:

"AI assists, but never overrides the system."

---

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ clicking randomly
- ❌ explaining code
- ❌ jumping between features
- ❌ showing unfinished flows

---

## Timing Rules

- Core demo: 5–7 minutes
- Executive demo: 3–5 minutes
- Deep dive: optional

---

## Final Rule

Every demo must lead to:

"I understand this, and I want it."
